Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Snore Fit works without an account, and your answers and progress stay on your phone.

Does the app record me while I sleep?

No. This version does not record audio or use the camera.

How do I change or turn off the daily reminder?

Open Settings in the app and change the reminder time, or switch the reminder off.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data, or Delete my data. Deleting removes everything the app has stored.

An exercise hurts. What should I do?

Stop the exercise. If you have a jaw joint (TMJ) problem or recent mouth or throat surgery, ask a doctor before continuing.

Can Snore Fit treat sleep apnea?

No. Snore Fit is a wellness app for everyday snoring, not a medical device. If you stop breathing in your sleep, wake up gasping, or are very sleepy during the day, see a doctor.
